# zara-sales-analysis-dashboard

## Overview

Power BI dashboard analyzing Zara's sales performance across products, pricing, promotions, placement, and product segments. 

## Objective

To analyze Zara's sales data, identify key performance trends, and provide insights that can support commercial decision-making.

## Tools Used

. Power BI
. Power Query
. DAX

## Key Insights

.Aisle placement led product placement performance with 177,396 units sold.
.Non-promoted products outperformed promoted products, recording a sales volume of 240,312 units.
.Seasonal products contributed 50.74% of total sales volume.
.The leading section(man) contributed 86.21% of the total sales volume.
.Medium-priced products led sales performance with 200,533 units sold.

## Recommendations
.Prioritize Jackets and Sweaters as proven high-performing products.
.Investigate the low sales performance of Jeans.
.Review promotional strategies based on actual sales results.
.Prioritize Aisle placement for overall sales volumes and strategically use Front Store placement for high-potential products.
.Maintain a balanced pricing strategy rather than assuming lower prices always drive higher sales.
